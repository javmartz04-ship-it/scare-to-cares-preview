The real problem

When the project starts slipping, your instinct is to take more control.

That may be exactly what's making it worse.

  • More meetings
  • More approvals
  • More oversight
  • More pressure
  1. 01

    The deadline slips.

    Stakeholders want answers, and the safest move feels like taking more control.

  2. 02

    Your team starts waiting for approval.

    Instead of deciding, they ask: "What does the leader want us to do?"

  3. 03

    The real problems arrive late.

    Trust gets thinner, and people stop telling leadership what it needs to hear.

  4. 04

    You become the bottleneck.

    The person carrying the most responsibility ends up carrying even more.

The technology isn't what's stalling your transformation. Fear is making the decisions.

Under pressure, good leaders can become the bottleneck they're trying to remove. Saby calls this SCARE. You can have the right platform, plan, vendor and people, and still watch a transformation stall because pressure changes how those people lead.
